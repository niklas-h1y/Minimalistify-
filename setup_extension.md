# How to Install the Extension

Since this extension is open-source, you can easily load it manually into your browser. Follow the instructions below for your specific device.

---

## 📱 Option A: Installation on Mobile (Android)
To run this extension on your phone, you need a browser that supports extension loading, like **Kiwi Browser** or **Mises Browser**.

### Step 1: Download the Files
1. On this GitHub repository page, tap the green **Code** button at the top.
2. Select **Download ZIP** and save it to your phone's storage.

### Step 2: Load into Kiwi Browser
1. Open **Kiwi Browser** on your smartphone.
2. Enter **`kiwi://extensions`** into the address bar and hit enter.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (toggle switch in the top-right corner).
4. Tap the **`+ (from .zip/.crx/.user.js)`** button.
5. Open your file manager, navigate to your Downloads folder, and select the downloaded ZIP file.

*The extension icon will now appear in your browser menu (the three dots in the top-right corner).*

---

## 💻 Option B: Installation on Desktop (PC / Mac)
Works on Google Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera, or any other Chromium-based browser.

### Step 1: Download and Extract
1. Click the green **Code** button at the top of this repository.
2. Click **Download ZIP**.
3. Extract the downloaded ZIP file into a folder on your computer.

### Step 2: Load into Chrome
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Navigate to **`chrome://extensions/`**.
3. Enable **Developer mode** by toggling the switch in the top-right corner.
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
5. Select the extracted folder (the one containing the `manifest.json` file).

---

## 🧪 How to Test It
1. Open a cluttered website with lots of text or blocks (e.g., Wikipedia, Reddit, or a news website).
2. Open the extension popup menu.
3. Click **Mild** to see the modern fonts and beautiful rounded corners, or **Hardcore** to remove all images and focus entirely on reading!
